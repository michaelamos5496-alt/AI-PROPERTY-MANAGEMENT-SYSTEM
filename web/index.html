Questions

Does the AI send messages to my tenants by itself?

No. It can draft a message. A person must request it and a different person with manager rights must approve it. Emailing or messaging tenants is off until you switch it on.

Which currencies and countries are supported?

Money is stored exactly in each currency's smallest unit. Ghana cedi, Nigerian naira, Kenyan shilling and US dollar are tested; other currencies work for ledgers. Online payment of your subscription depends on what our payment provider enables for your currency, and is not switched on yet; we assign plans by hand during early access.

Do my tenants need an app?

No. They use a web page or WhatsApp. A tenant's WhatsApp number is linked to them with a one-time code you give them, so a phone number alone never unlocks anyone's account details.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. You keep read and edit access to what you have; you simply can't add more than the free plan allows until you choose a plan again.

Is it production-ready?

It is in early access. The core is tested heavily, and we are completing verification with each external provider and independent security review before we call it generally available. Ask us for the current status.
